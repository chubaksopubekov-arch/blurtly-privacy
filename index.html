Otvio — Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 14, 2026 · Last updated: November 9, 2026

The short version: Otvio stores everything on your device and sends nothing anywhere. We have no servers, no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no third-party SDKs. In App Store terms, Otvio's privacy label is "Data Not Collected" — under Apple's definition, data that is processed only on your device is not "collected", and that is exactly how Otvio works.

What this policy covers

This policy covers the Otvio iOS app, its keyboard extension, and this web page. This page is a static document: it sets no cookies and runs no analytics or scripts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; like any web host, GitHub may keep its own standard server logs — see GitHub's privacy statement.

What data Otvio handles

Otvio works with the following data, all of which stays on your device:

  • Your snippets and folders — the text templates you create.
  • Clipboard history ("Last Copies") — the most recent texts you copied: the last 10 items on the free plan, the last 50 with Otvio Pro.
  • Documents and attachments — files (PDF, images) you add to the app or attach to snippets.
  • Usage statistics and business events — which of your snippets was inserted and when, together with the meaning you assigned to it (for example "Sale" or "New inquiry"). This powers the Insights and Statistics screens.
  • App settings — such as enabled keyboard languages, frequently used emoji, and your report preferences.

This data is stored in the app's private container on your device (shared between the app and its keyboard via Apple's App Group mechanism). It is never transmitted to us or to anyone else. There is no Otvio server it could be sent to.

The keyboard and Full Access

Otvio's keyboard requests the iOS "Allow Full Access" permission for one purpose only: reading your clipboard to build the "Last Copies" history (10 items free / 50 with Pro).

  • The keyboard makes no network connections at all — with or without Full Access. Although Full Access technically permits network use, Otvio's keyboard never uses it: there is nothing to connect to.
  • The keyboard does not log, store, or transmit what you type. The only activity it records is the fact that your own snippet was inserted — the local counter behind your own Statistics and Insights screens.
  • The clipboard is read only at the moment the keyboard or the app is opened — iOS does not allow background clipboard monitoring, and we do not attempt it.
  • Items marked as concealed by password managers are never saved to history.
  • Word suggestions are computed entirely on your device using Apple's built-in dictionaries and bundled word-frequency lists. The word you are typing is processed in memory only — it is never saved and never sent anywhere.
  • Snippet insertion works even if Full Access is turned off.

Clipboard access in the app

When you open the Otvio app, it automatically adds the current clipboard text to your history — this is what keeps "Last Copies" useful, and it happens right away, without a prompt on every copy. iOS governs this with its system permission: the first time, iOS asks "Allow Paste from Other Apps?", and your choice (Allow / Deny / Ask every time) applies from then on. You can change it at any time in iOS Settings → Otvio → Paste from Other Apps.

Photos and files

When you attach an image, Otvio uses the system photo picker: the app receives only the photos you explicitly pick and has no access to the rest of your photo library. Files are added through the system document picker in the same way.

Everything below lives on your device only, and all of it is erased if you delete the app.

Insights and exports

All analytics in Otvio (Insights, Statistics, reports) are computed on your device from the local data described above. Numbers count snippet insertions — Otvio never reads your conversations and cannot see your chats.

You can export your own data as a report (PDF), a spreadsheet (Excel) or a CSV file. An export is created only when you tap the export button, and it leaves your device only through the standard iOS share sheet — to the destination you choose. We never receive a copy. If you choose to share an export with a third-party service (for example an AI assistant), that service's own privacy policy applies to the file you give it.

Purchases (Otvio Pro)

Otvio offers an optional subscription, Otvio Pro. Payments are processed entirely by Apple through your Apple ID: we never see or store your payment details, and no Otvio account is created. The only thing stored on your device is whether the subscription is active, so the app and keyboard know which features to unlock. You can manage or cancel the subscription anytime in your App Store settings, and restore a previous purchase with "Restore Purchases". Apple is the only third party involved in running Otvio, and its handling of purchases is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

What we never do

  • We do not collect, sell, or share any personal data.
  • We do not use analytics, tracking, or advertising of any kind.
  • We do not require registration or accounts.
  • We do not include any third-party SDKs in the app.
  • We have no servers — there is nowhere for your data to go.

Your data, your controls

Because everything is local, every control is in your hands:

  • Full Access — turn it off anytime (iOS Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards → Otvio). The keyboard keeps inserting snippets; only clipboard history stops updating.
  • Clipboard capture in the app — allow, deny, or require a prompt in iOS Settings → Otvio → Paste from Other Apps.
  • Word suggestions — switch off in Otvio → Settings → Word suggestions.
  • Delete anything — snippets, folders, history items, documents, and custom statuses can be deleted in the app; "Delete Now" skips the 30-day trash.
  • Delete everything — removing the Otvio app from your device permanently erases all of its data: snippets, history, statistics, and the archive. Exports you saved elsewhere remain wherever you put them.

Since we hold no copy of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side — and nothing we could hand over, lose, or have breached.

Your rights (GDPR, CCPA and similar laws)

Privacy laws such as the EU GDPR and the California CCPA give you rights over personal data that companies process about you. Otvio's design makes most of them automatic: we do not process your personal data on any system of ours, so there is nothing for us to access, correct, export, or erase — your device holds the only copy, under your control, with the tools described above. If you believe we hold any personal data about you (for example, because you emailed us), contact us and we will resolve it.

Children

Otvio is a business tool and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Otvio adds features that change how data is handled (for example, optional cloud sync), this policy will be updated before that version is released, the "Last updated" date above will change, and the app will ask for any newly required permissions explicitly.
